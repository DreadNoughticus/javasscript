More Use Cases and Why They Are Better Than Centralized Solutions

Mesh networks offer several advantages over centralized solutions. Here are some examples:

  • Resilience: Mesh networks are more resilient to failures and disruptions. If one node fails, the network can still function as long as there is an alternative path.
  • Scalability: Mesh networks can scale more easily than centralized solutions. Adding new nodes to the network is straightforward and does not require significant changes to the existing infrastructure.
  • Cost-Effectiveness: Mesh networks can be more cost-effective than centralized solutions. They require less cabling and hardware, and they can be deployed more quickly and easily.
  • Flexibility: Mesh networks offer more flexibility than centralized solutions. They can be configured to meet specific requirements and can be easily adapted to changing needs.

These advantages make mesh networks a compelling choice for a wide range of applications.
